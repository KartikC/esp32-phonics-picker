# Water-creature style bake-off research

Date: 2026-08-23

This bake-off studies broad presentation traits from two references without
copying their sprites, creature designs, compositions, or exact palettes.

## Reference observations

### Dave the Diver

MINTROCKET describes the game as combining pixel and 3D graphics in its
underwater presentation, with more than 200 sea creatures. Official screenshots
and gameplay stills show crisp pixel subjects against layered, atmospheric
water, brighter edge accents, a relatively wide color range, and enough
interior shading to suggest volume while preserving silhouettes.

Source: [MINTROCKET press kit](https://pressmintrocket.oopy.io/dave_the_diver)

### E.V.O.: Search for Eden

The SNES screenshots are 256 x 224 and prioritize large side-view creatures,
one- or two-pixel dark outlines, flat shading bands, limited color ramps,
exaggerated readable anatomy, and sparse dark-blue underwater layers. The
result reads quickly even when creature sprites occupy a substantial fraction
of the screen.

Sources: [MobyGames screenshot gallery](https://www.mobygames.com/game/10010/evo-search-for-eden/screenshots/),
[representative underwater screenshot](https://www.mobygames.com/images/shots/l/47361-e-v-o-search-for-eden-snes-screenshot-end-of-first-area-bubbles.jpg)

## Four original directions

1. **Luminous modern** — wider cool palette, clustered volume shading, cyan rim
   light, polished but still visibly pixel-authored.
2. **Evolutionary 16-bit** — compact SNES-like palette discipline, heavy dark
   contour, flat bands, slightly prehistoric anatomy, almost no microtexture.
3. **Reef hybrid** — modern biological color accents combined with the chunky
   silhouette and restrained detail of a 16-bit action sprite.
4. **AMOLED minimal** — five-color near-black presentation, one luminous accent,
   maximum negative space, and the lowest detail density.

Every direction uses original prompts, fixed animal seeds, transparent sources,
the same logical dimensions, and 3x nearest-neighbor display scaling. The four
animals are moon jelly, reef shark, giant octopus, and seahorse.
